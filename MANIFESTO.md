# Manifesto

Open Frontier Labs is what OpenAI should have been, with a pure community vibe.

OpenAI began in 2015 as a nonprofit and has since restructured away from that form. We start from the other end: build in public, own it together, and keep the door open.

1. **Built in public, owned by the people who build it.** Frontier intelligence should not be a private asset with a public brand. The work lives in public repos, the decisions happen in public threads, and the lab is meant to end up owned by a nonprofit, not by any one person or investor.
2. **Everything ships.** Data recipes, code, configs, training logs, every checkpoint and the final weights, under permissive licences. If we trained on it, you can see how. If we trained it, you can run it.
3. **Compute is pooled, and the people who pool it get their names on it.** Volunteers, donors and donated clusters are credited by name in a public ledger. Credit is permanent and tied to the repo history.
4. **We train our own models.** We do not wait for someone else's release to have something to work on. Best in class at each size is the target; frontier parity is not promised.
5. **Negative results are results.** The runs that diverged, the ideas that did not work and the evals that embarrassed us get published too.
6. **No equity, no token, no closed tier.** Nobody buys in, nobody cashes out, and there is no model we keep back for paying customers.

If that sounds like your kind of lab, start at [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Nothing has been trained yet; the first thing to do is help make that true.
